still can't find it?☏ 1-800-RIP-DATA

01Can you bring it back?

No. That's the whole point. We are a funeral home, not a backup service. If you want it back, you didn't want a funeral, you wanted a nap.

02Can I attend my own account's funeral?

Most people do. Sit in the back. Wear sunglasses. Nobody will say anything, except Cache.

03What happens to the comments?

They are read aloud during the service. All of them. Yes, even yours from 2012. Especially yours from 2012.

04Do you do pets?

Only virtual ones. Biscuit's service is still the most attended in the history of the house.

05What if someone else has the password?

Then it isn't dead, it's haunted. We offer a separate service for that. It involves a candle, a new email address and a long walk.

06Is a group chat one funeral or eleven?

One funeral, eleven mourners, at least two of them pretending they never muted it.
