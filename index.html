02 / who we are

Nothing online is forever. Profiles go quiet, chats get muted, blogs stop loading, save files corrupt. We make sure every one of them gets a goodbye.

Since 2009 the Vale family has laid to rest more than 0 accounts, 0 group chats and one entire social network (we don't talk about it).

We are not a backup service. We don't recover, restore or reactivate. We hold the door, dim the lights and say the name out loud, one last time.
